# Getting help

- **Documentation:** https://docs.mystartica.com, including install, first run, bring-your-own credentials and troubleshooting.
- **Questions and ideas:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/chriscasper/startica/discussions).
- **Bugs:** [open an issue](https://github.com/chriscasper/startica/issues/new/choose) with your OS, the Startica version or commit, and the relevant logs.
- **Security problems:** don't open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Anything else:** hello@mystartica.com.

Startica is maintained in spare time, so answers can take a few days.
