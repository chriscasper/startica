# Trademark policy

Startica's code is free under the [MIT License](LICENSE): use it, change it, share it and build on it, for any purpose. The license covers the code only. It doesn't cover the **Startica** name, the Startica logo, the S mark or the app icon (the "marks"), which belong to Christopher Casper.

This policy explains how you can use the marks. The goal is simple: anyone should be able to tell whether they're getting Startica from this project or something else.

## Fine without asking

- **Saying what's true.** Writing about Startica, reviewing it, teaching it, or saying that your product, plugin or service works with Startica ("a channel adapter for Startica", "works with Startica").
- **Linking to the project** with the name or the logo, following the logo guidelines on the [brand page](https://mystartica.com/brand).
- **Unchanged builds.** Sharing copies built from this repository without changes, under the Startica name.
- **Your own fork, for yourself.** Changing the code and running it for yourself or inside your own organization, even if it still says Startica.

## Needs your own name

If you share or sell a modified version, or offer Startica as a hosted or paid service, give it a different name and logo:

- Don't call it Startica, or a name that could be confused with it ("Startica Pro", "Startika").
- Remove the Startica logo, S mark and app icon, and change the app's name, window titles and installer.
- You may say it's based on Startica: "Built on Startica" or "a fork of Startica" is fine, as long as it doesn't suggest this project made or endorses it.

## Never

- Suggesting that this project, or Christopher Casper, makes, sponsors or endorses your product or service when it doesn't.
- Using the marks in a domain name, app store listing, social account or company name in a way that could be mistaken for the official project.
- Changing the logo's shape, adding effects, or using it as your own product's logo.
