## Now

- **Windows code signing:** installers for macOS, Windows and Linux are published on GitHub Releases, and installed copies update themselves. The Mac builds are signed and notarized; the Windows installer isn't code-signed yet. Windows and Linux builds also need more testing.
- **Testing on real sites and accounts:** every module is tested against stand-ins. Next is real sites, real Search Console properties, real posts on each of the seven posting sites, and Windows and Linux desktops. Reports are especially welcome.

## Next

Nothing is queued right now. What comes next is picked from the list below and from what people ask for in Discussions.

## Later

- **Marketing:** more channels (such as Threads and YouTube comments), and reading ad spend from the ad platforms instead of entering it by hand.
- **SEO & GEO:** redirect chains, page depth and `hreflang`; queries with no matching page; GitHub and GitLab issues for search findings.
- **Accessibility:** scans behind a sign-in, mobile viewports and a keyboard walk-through.
- **Security:** more library detection, and reading PDF reports from other vendors.
- **Ideas from the community:** if something here matters to you, or is missing, say so in Discussions.

## Recently shipped

- **Security behind a sign-in:** the same passive checks on the pages you list behind your sign-in, after you sign in by hand, plus no-scan windows to keep scans away from busy hours.
- **Cheaper bulk AI:** large scoring backlogs go through Anthropic's and OpenAI's batch APIs, at about half the price.
- **Accessibility extras:** ignoring an issue on one page only, a score weighted by traffic, an HTML accessibility statement to paste into your site, and a PDF of the ACR draft.
- **AI visibility with both providers:** Claude and OpenAI asked in the same run, and results broken down by topic.
- **Refine in more places:** chat with the AI to revise SEO drafts, audit findings and roadmap items, and ask about a triage, a review or an AI answer.
- **More color and dark mode:** each module has its own accent, and the app follows your system's light or dark setting.
- **Open source:** the repository is public, with documentation, contributor guides, CI and the Fieldnote demo product to try the app with.
- **Security:** ownership verification, outside checks on every scan, exposure monitoring, AI help and a disclosure kit.
- **SEO & GEO:** site health, Search Console and Bing data, AI visibility, ranked opportunities, agent readiness and off-site presence.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG scans with axe-core, issues ranked by impact, AI help, a manual checklist, reports, and content and PDF checks.
- **Ads:** plans, copy, images and results for Google, Meta and Reddit, with suggestions for what to do next.
- **The Dashboard**, a background status indicator, OpenAI as a second AI provider, and Umami analytics.

Everything else is in the [changelog](/changelog).
